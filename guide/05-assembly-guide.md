## Workshop assembly overview

The presentation plans for participants to practice soldering in small groups, then work on a pedal PCB with organizer assistance and test the result with a guitar.

1. **Identify your parts.** Review the PCB labels and the **organizer-approved component list and placement guide**.
2. **Check component orientation.** Capacitors, diodes, LEDs, sockets, and some ICs have specific polarities or orientations. Ask the instructor before soldering.
3. **Practice first.** Make a few clean joints on the designated practice materials.
4. **Solder the PCB.** Have an instructor supervise placement and inspect joints as you go.
5. **Fit the electronics into the pedal enclosure.** Follow the instructor's mechanical assembly instructions; protect the board from shorts.
6. **Check before power.** Instructors should check continuity, shorts, power, orientation, and firmware before powering the build.
7. **Test the effect.** Once an organizer confirms the pedal is safe and working, try it with a guitar and amplifier.

### Important: read before attempting assembly

The supplied files **do not contain a verified bill of materials, solder-by-reference instructions, firmware, or prototype test results**. Consequently this page is an **event workflow**, not a complete component-by-component assembly manual. The organizers must add an approved placement diagram, part inventory and functional test checklist before participants build independently.
