## What is soldering?

Soldering joins metal surfaces by melting a filler metal called **solder**. On a PCB, solder makes electrical and mechanical connections between a component lead and a copper pad.

## ⚠️ Safety first

- Follow your workshop instructor; solder only at supervised stations.
- Wear safety glasses and use the station's approved fume extraction.
- The tip is hot: hold the iron by its insulated handle and return it to its stand.
- Keep the work area clear; never solder a powered circuit.
- Wash your hands after handling solder, especially if it contains lead.
- The instructor will set the temperature appropriate for the solder and equipment; **375°C is not a universal required setting**.

## How to solder a through-hole component

1. **Prepare:** Confirm the part, orientation, and soldering point with an instructor. Insert the lead into its hole.
2. **Heat the joint:** Touch the iron tip to the copper pad and component lead together.
3. **Apply solder:** Feed a small amount of solder to the heated joint, not simply onto the iron tip.
4. **Remove:** Pull away solder wire, then remove the iron.
5. **Cool:** Allow the joint to solidify without moving the part.
6. **Inspect:** Look for a smooth joint and check for unwanted bridges to nearby pads. Ask an instructor to check uncertain joints.
