# 🎸 UH SASE Guitar Pedal Soldering Workshop

Welcome and thank you for coming to the **UH SASE Guitar Pedal Soldering Workshop**! Follow these pages during the event to learn how a digital delay pedal works, practice soldering, explore a custom PCB, and personalize a pedal design.

> **Before the event:** Our organizers will confirm the soldering demonstration video, materials, assembly instructions, and how to etch. The provided electronics files below are just design references.

## 🛠 Workshop flow

1. Learn what a guitar pedal is and the controls we are focusing on today. 
2. Practice soldering with leds.
3. Practice ethcing.
4. When ready, begin soldering the PCB **only with an instructor present!**
5. Assemble the PCB
6. Test the pedal with a guitar and amplifier!.

## ⚠️ Safety precautions

A hot soldering iron can cause burns; use gloves, goggles and fume extractor. 


**Questions?** Ask a UH SASE workshop organizer.
