# 🎸 UH SASE Guitar Pedal Soldering Workshop

Welcome to the **UH SASE Guitar Pedal Soldering Workshop**! Follow these pages during the event to learn how a digital delay pedal works, practice soldering, explore a custom PCB, and personalize a pedal design.

> **Before the event:** Our organizers will confirm the soldering demonstration video, materials, assembly instructions, and etching method. The provided electronics files are design references, **not a verified, ready-to-build kit**.

## 🛠 Workshop flow

1. Learn what a delay effect is.
2. Practice soldering at a supervised station.
3. Practice the organizer-approved design/etching activity.
4. Assemble the PCB **only with an instructor and the verified build instructions**.
5. Test an instructor-verified pedal with a guitar and amplifier.

## ⚠️ Safety and project status

A hot soldering iron can cause burns; follow instructor instructions, use appropriate fume extraction and eye protection, and never work on a powered circuit. Etching details depend on the surface, method, and available safety equipment.

**Not included in the supplied hardware design files:** validated firmware, a confirmed bill of materials, verified component-by-component assembly placements, and a working prototype test report. The organizers must verify these before treating the design as a complete build.

**Event questions?** Ask a UH SASE workshop organizer.
