## What is soldering?

Soldering joins metal surfaces by melting a filler metal called **solder**. On a PCB, solder makes electrical and mechanical connections between a component lead and a copper pad.

## ⚠️ Safety first

- Follow your workshop instructor.
- Wear safety glasses, gloves, and use the station's approved fume extraction.
- The tip is hot: hold the iron by its insulated handle and return it to its stand.
- Keep the work area clear; never solder a powered circuit.
- Wash your hands after handling solder, especially if it contains lead.

## How to Solder

1. Set the soldering device to about 375 degrees C.
2. Use your dominant hand to hold the soldering machine.
3. Lightly place the tip on the circuit board, and lighly tap the metal onto the tip as indicated in the video.
4. After each soldering round, wait ~3 seconds.
