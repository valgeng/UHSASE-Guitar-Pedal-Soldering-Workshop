# 01 — What is a guitar pedal?

[← Workshop home](../README.md) · [Next: Pedal controls →](02-pedal-controls.md)

A **guitar pedal** is an electronic device placed between a guitar and amplifier that changes the guitar's sound. There are many effects, such as distortion, reverb, chorus, and delay.

**Our workshop focuses on a delay pedal.** 

**Basic signal path:** `Guitar → Pedal → Amplifier`

## Listen for the difference

- **Without delay:** One note produces its original sound.
- **With delay:** You hear the note, then one or more repeats after it.
